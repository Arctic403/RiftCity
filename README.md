What RiftCity V1 should contain

1. A strong home/dashboard

* Character/profile
* Level, XP, health, energy
* Cash + bank
* Current location
* Notifications/events
* Quick actions
* Daily objectives
* A rotating “City Feed” showing things happening around RiftCity

It should feel like you’re looking at your life inside the city, not an admin panel.

⸻

🗺️ 2. An actual city

Instead of Torn’s huge list of menus, make the city itself the organizing principle.

For example:

RiftCity

* 🏠 Home
* 🏦 Downtown
* 🛒 Market District
* 🔫 Industrial District
* 🏢 Financial District
* 🌆 Nightlife
* 🚓 Police District
* 🏥 Medical
* 🏚️ The Underground

Each area should have its own identity, activities, NPCs and risks.

3. The core progression loop

V1 needs a satisfying loop:

Do things → earn money/XP → upgrade → unlock new opportunities → take bigger risks → become powerful.

Examples:

Early game

* Small jobs
* Street fights
* Deliveries
* Gambling/minigames
* Scavenging
* Basic businesses

Mid game

* Heists
* Criminal organizations
* Investments
* Properties
* Better equipment
* Territory

Late game

* Major businesses
* City influence
* Elite crimes
* Factions
* Political/economic manipulation
* Endgame achievements

⸻

👤 4. Make the player matter

This is probably the biggest difference I’d make from classic Torn.

Your character should have an actual identity.

Things like:

* Stats
* Skills
* Reputation
* Alignment/reputation with factions
* Inventory
* Equipment
* Properties
* Businesses
* Achievements
* Titles
* Criminal record
* Relationships

And importantly:

Choices should change your experience.

If I become a respected businessman, RiftCity should feel different than if I become a notorious street criminal.

⸻

🎮 5. Make activities fun individually

Don’t make every feature:

Click button → wait 60 seconds → number goes up.

V1 should have several activities with actual interaction.

For example:

Combat

* Choose attack
* Defend
* Use abilities/items
* Different enemy types
* Equipment matters

Heists

* Choose approach
* Risk/reward
* Random events
* Skill checks
* Consequences

Jobs

* Different jobs with different requirements
* Short-term vs long-term jobs
* Career progression

Market

* Buy/sell
* Player economy eventually
* Fluctuating prices

⸻

🧠 6. NPCs before multiplayer

This is important for the direction you already described.

Don’t wait for multiplayer to make RiftCity feel alive.

Give the player:

* Shopkeepers
* Fixers
* Police
* Doctors
* Criminals
* Business owners
* Faction leaders
* Rivals
* Random citizens
* Quest NPCs

They can have dialogue, personalities and recurring interactions.

Then multiplayer eventually adds real players into an already-living world.

⸻

🏆 7. V1 needs goals

A new player should immediately understand:

“What am I trying to accomplish?”

Give them:

* Main progression
* Missions
* Achievements
* Daily/weekly objectives
* Collection goals
* Businesses to unlock
* Areas to unlock
* Reputation milestones
* Character levels
* Long-term challenges

You want the player constantly thinking:

“Okay… just one more upgrade.”

⸻

🔥 The V1 feature priority I’d use

Tier 1 — MUST HAVE

* Character system
* Dashboard
* City/navigation
* Economy
* Jobs
* Combat
* Inventory
* Equipment
* Health/energy
* XP/levels
* Missions
* NPCs
* Basic businesses

Tier 2 — Make it addictive

* Properties
* Factions
* Reputation
* Heists

Tier 3 — Multiplayer foundation

* Player profiles

⸻

🌆 And visually…

I’d not make RiftCity look like a Torn clone.

I’d go for:

Dark + neon + gritty + modern + game-like.

Think:

GTA-style city atmosphere + cyberpunk UI + RPG progression + Torn’s persistent-world mechanics.

The dashboard shouldn’t look like spreadsheets.

It should look like you’re logged into your character’s life.

And that’s the direction I’d take the repo toward before touching the backend heavily:

V1 = a ridiculously polished single-player crime/RPG experience.

Then multiplayer becomes the thing that makes an already-good game explode in depth, rather than being the thing that makes the game interesting in the first place.
